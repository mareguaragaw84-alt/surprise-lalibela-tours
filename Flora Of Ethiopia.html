flora of Ethiopia

         The flora of Ethiopia is very diverse with an estimated number 7,000 species of higher plants, About 15 % or more are endemic Ethiopia is considered as the fifth largest floral country in tropical Africa About 300 tree species represent the floral diversity The horn of Africa (Ethiopia, Eritrea, Djibouti and Somalia) harboring more than 8000 plant species, About 24 %endemic to the region (Friis et al., 2003).

Afro alpine vegetation

        These can be further sub-divided into ten broad ecosystems: Afro-alpine and sub-alpine Dry evergreen montane forest and grassland Moist evergreen montane forest Moist evergreen lowland forest Congo-Guinean forest Acacia woodland and thickets Acacia-Commiphora woodland Combretum-Terminalia woodland/savannah Lakes, wetlands & river systems Arid ecosystems Alpine vegetationisfound Above the tree line on mountains above 2,500 metre. Alpine lakes rich in high land biomes Hosts 5 large endemic mammals & 1 burrowing E rodent Dominated by grass and Helichrysum splendidum.

Protected areas of Ethiopia

         An area of land or sea especially dedicated to the protection and maintenance of biological diversity and of natural and or associated cultural resources and managed through legal and other effective means. Major management purpose includes: Scientific research Protecting wilderness Preserving species and genetic diversity Maintain eco-system services Protecting specific cultural and natural features Tourism and recreational Education and Sustainable use of the resources from natural eco-systems.
