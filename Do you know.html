Month by month
Top events

January

The most vibrant and busy time to visit, with Ethiopias most colorful festival usually cool and dry weather.

Ledet (Christmas)
Ledet (6 to7 January) is a dramatic throwback to a time the faithful attend all-night services, Lalibela are the most exciting time to visit during the major festival, thousands crowd of pilgrims crown in. Timket and ledet are the biggest, but Lalibela draws masses for all other major ones.

Timkat(celebrating Christs baptism)
(19, 20,21 January)this three-day festival is the most vibrant of the year. Thousands of people sing and dance behind a solemn procession of regalia-draped priests.

February

The heart of the dry season is the easiest time to travel around Ethiopia and its a good time for trekking, wildlife-watching and driving.

March

The end of the high season. days are warm and dry, theres excellent wildlife waching around waterholes in the national parks.
Bull-jumping ceremony

In the lower omo valley, witness a young man run across the backs of cattle to be initiated into the responsibilites of manhood, while,in a show of solidarity and stoic allegiance, women volunteer to be whipped with slender canes. Between late January and early April.


April

A transition month. Increasing the temperatures in the Ethiopian lowlands are mitigated by heavy showers.
Good Friday
From Thursday evening befor Good Friday, the faithful fast until Easter service which ends at 3am on Easter Sunday. Held in March or April.
Fasika( Orthodox Easter)
Fasika marks the end of a vegetarian fast of 55 days. Stay up on the night of Easter Saturday in LALIBELA to see hudreds of white-robed pligrims crowd the courtyards of the churches and pray unde the moonlight.

May
Pessimists decry the beginning of the rainy season. But the region is green and lush. Avoid the Lower Omo Valley as many road are impassable.


August
                                                                  The big annual rains continue to better Ethiopia.

September
Kidus Yohannes ( New Year�s Day)
At Ethiopia New year( 11 September), new clothes are traditionally bought for the occasion for children, and particularly for relatives and frinds are visited.

Meskel (Finding of the True Cross)
Starting on 27 September,this tow-day festival is the most colourful bonfire are built, topped by a cross to which flower, most commonly the meskel daisy, are tied. Priests don their full regalia.

October
Mid-october is a great time to visit Ethiopia the countryside glow green.
Irecha
The oromo people celebrat Irecha on the shores of the Lake Hora.Devotees gather around ancent. Fig trees to smear perfume,butter and katickala ( a distilled drink) on the trunks and share ceremonial meals of roasted meat, coffee and alcohol.

November
A great time to visit expect plenty of wildlife in the national parks and migratory birds begin arriving in great numbers. Festival of Maryam Zion< This vibrant festival is held only in Aksum. In the days leading up to the event on 30 November,thousands of pilgrims head towards Aksumn. Celebrations start in front to the Northern stelae field, where the monarchs of the Orthodox church line the steps.

December
The weather is mostly dry thoughout the region and it�s a fine time for wildlife watching. Temprerratures are also at their best warm but not stifiling.this is a great time to explore the Danakil Depression.
Kulbi Gabriel
Although not on the official religious holidays list, large numbers of Ethiopians make a pilgrimage to the venerated kulbi Gabriel church, near Dire Dawa in the east.
