        Bete Giorgis is decorated by two rows of windows.The nine windows of the bottom row are blind and imitate the Aksumite monkey-head framework.Twelve windows of the upper row have ogive arches and bracket capitals.these are very similar to some the window in bete Golgotha.As usual, bet Giorgis has three doors in the west,north and south.The main enterance in the west is approached by seven wide steps.The main door way has a shallow porch ornamented by simple moldings.

Bete mariam
          bete mariam is a centeral church in the first group surrouded by a trapezoid shaped courtyard. The main access to the church is through a narrow corridor that starts from the western face of Bete medhane Alem.It is a rectangular church.from the exterior,moldings running horizontally around the walls decorate the church.

Bete Gebriel

         Bete Gabriel is a church located b/n two trenches on the north and south.Access to the church from the near by rock gallery is over a long brige crossing the main trech.The monumental northern facade of Bete Gabriel is the most impressive feature.


Bete medhane Alem

         Bete medhane Alem is the first church from the first group of the whole complex that vistors access at the beginning from the town center.presently, the main access to the church is through astaircase at the northwestern corner.Ahuge courtyard surrounds it.The church is rectangular in shape and is the largest.It is perhaps, the largest rock-hewn churchin Ethiopia.


Bete Amanuel

         Bete Amanuel is the only purely monolithic and carfully hewn church of the second group at a center of a quadrilateral like courtyard.It is a rectangular church.The External facade of Bete Amanuel shows the aksumit style of interchargeing recessed and projecting stonework.
